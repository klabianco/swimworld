# Swimworld

A 3D swimming game made by Vienna and her dad. Play it at https://klabianco.github.io/swimworld/

Swim from Laguna Beach through real ocean places on Earth to find your hidden house. Works in the browser on iPad, phones and laptops.
Art credits: see CREDITS.md (all CC0 from Poly Haven).

Surfing: swim to the ocean surface and tap **Surf** (or press **B**). The board points with the swell. Use WASD, arrow keys, or the joystick to paddle and carve; a passing wave catches the board automatically. Tap the button again or hold Dive to return to swimming. The button shows the distance you have surfed this session.

The ocean has traveling swells, textured whitecaps, crest lighting, and extra mesh detail near the player. Surface daylight stays bright over deep ocean regions; diving still darkens the scene.

Run the surfing physics checks with `node tests/surf-physics.cjs`.
