A swimming simulator

Swimworld

Your own island in a real ocean. Build your dream house, then swim out to explore real places on Earth, find treasure, go on quests and make friends.

  • SwimUse the joystick, or W A S D / arrow keys, to swim. Hold Space or Dive to go under. Drag the screen to look around.
  • Stay aliveKeep moving or you'll sink. Come up for air. Stay away from sharks and red jellyfish.
  • Build & explorePress Build on your island to place blocks. Press T to talk to people and animals, and M for your map.
  • Coins & XPGrab pearls and shells, open treasure chests, talk to people and find new places. Coins buy gear for every animal at the Dive Shop, and you can upgrade each piece, level by level, to swim faster and hold your breath longer. Every Turbo Booster doubles your speed again! XP makes you faster and unlocks new swimmers: turtle, otter, dolphin.

Continue logs you in and loads your saved swimmer.
